## 🎯 About

**QNow** is a smart queue management system designed specifically for Algeria that transforms how people experience waiting in lines. Built with modern technology using Flutter and Dart, QNow provides a seamless cross-platform experience for both customers and businesses.

The app enables users to:
- Join virtual queues from anywhere
- Track their position in real-time
- Receive notifications when their turn approaches
- Manage their time more efficiently

For businesses, QNow offers:
- Efficient queue management tools
- Customer flow analytics
- Reduced physical crowding
- Improved customer satisfaction

### Why QNow?

In Algeria, long waiting times at government offices, banks, hospitals, and service centers are a common challenge. QNow addresses this problem by digitizing the queue experience, allowing people to wait virtually while going about their daily activities.

## ❓ FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is QNow free to use?**  
A: Yes! QNow is completely free for customers. You can join unlimited queues and receive notifications at no cost. Business owners also get free access to basic queue management features.

**Q: What languages does QNow support?**  
A: QNow supports three languages: English, French (Français), and Arabic (العربية). You can easily switch between languages in the app settings.

**Q: Do I need an internet connection?**  
A: Yes, QNow requires an active internet connection (WiFi or mobile data) for real-time updates. You can view your current position offline, but won't receive real-time updates until you reconnect.

### Using QNow

**Q: How do I join a queue?**  
A: Browse available queues, tap on the one you want to join, and tap "Join Queue". You'll receive a position number and can track your spot in real-time.

**Q: Can I leave a queue after joining?**  
A: Yes! Go to "My Queues", select the queue you want to leave, and tap "Leave Queue". There's no penalty for leaving.

**Q: What happens if I miss my turn?**  
A: You'll have a grace period (usually 5-10 minutes) to arrive. If you can't make it, you may need to rejoin the queue. Keep notifications enabled to avoid missing your turn.

**Q: How accurate is the wait time estimate?**  
A: QNow provides real-time estimates based on queue length and average service time. While we strive for accuracy, actual times may vary based on service complexity.

### Business Owners

**Q: Can I use QNow for my business?**  
A: Absolutely! Switch to Business Owner mode during signup or in your profile settings. You can create multiple queues, track customer flow, and access analytics for free.

**Q: How do I manage notifications to customers?**  
A: The app automatically sends notifications to customers as their turn approaches. You can also manually notify customers through the business dashboard.

### Security & Privacy

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: Yes! QNow uses industry-standard encryption and stores your data securely. We only collect essential information (name, phone, optional email) and never share your data with third parties.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**  
A: Yes, you can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app settings.

### Technical Support

**Q: How do I report a problem?**  
A: Contact us via email at teamqnow@gmail.com, call +213 553 72 90 19, or use the "Help & Support" section in the app. We typically respond within 24 hours.

**Q: What devices are supported?**  
A: Currently, QNow is available for Android devices. iOS support is coming soon!
